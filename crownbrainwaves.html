Project Overview

During my Advanced Game Design semester, in collaboration with my project group, we've made the decision to establish our own hypothetical game studio named Studio 21.

The scope of our project is very liberal - we have been instructed simply to figure out a way to implement the Neurosity brainwave crown into a Unity project of choice. As a result, we have decided to work on a walking simulator where the landscape you walk through changes based on the brainwaves the Crown is picking up – without assigning it to specific feelings to avoid any direct psychological analysis that might cause the player's landscape to change rapidly.  

My Role and Responsibilities

  • Scrum Master: Project time management and retrospectives, making use of a jira board.
  • Researching: Researching similar products that use EEG sensors and looking at interaction in walking simulators.
  • Developer Adding interaction in our walking simulator.
